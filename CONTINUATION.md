# Keep a conversation going

Version 0.8.8-preview.1. The thought cloud beside your message fills with the app's estimated input use. Its number is in characters, not tokenizer counts. Click it for the current account, original sources and preparation controls. A known local token window is distinguished from an unknown connection capacity.

Prepare an account of earlier complete exchanges while retaining the originals. The recorder gets the selected sources and account-writing task, with no action tools and none of your unsent draft. A valid result waits until the next reply is compiled. Review-before-use is optional. Both chairs use the same account. New messages after the frozen cutoff remain verbatim, including corrections. A completed summary is fallible; open its sources or save a correction when needed.

Choose either current chair for a participant account, or another saved model for an outside review. The groups appear in both Conversation settings and the manual preparation panel. The outside recorder does not join a chair. Its exact connection, role and source range are recorded; it receives no action tools. Both perspectives preserve original attribution and use the same shared account. A missing or changed recorder is held for review rather than silently replaced. Older neutral accounts keep their recorded meaning.

Compatible HTTP connections can reply during preparation. The included local runner, LM Studio and Codex use serial access. Preparation requested during a reply queues behind it; the queue expires after five minutes. Wait or Stop rather than assuming that a queue means simultaneous inference. A provider may serialize HTTP requests itself.

Settings → Conversation offers automatic preparation (off initially), the recorder, optional review, an optional near-limit notice and the cloud count. Enabling automatic preparation authorizes account-writing calls on that selected connection; they can consume its subscription or API allowance. There is at most one attempt per eligible source cutoff and a configurable timeout. No other model is substituted. Changing the recorder connection requires saving the choice again. Stop pauses automatic preparation persistently; explicitly enable it again to resume.

Changing models intentionally selects that model’s usual Coat or its saved branch override. Earlier replies keep their exact Coat version, original attribution, decisions, corrections, sources and unfinished questions. The destination must pass sharing review and fit the current input. This does not train either model, create new permission, or alter a Coat. Current instructions and pockets remain under your control. If a candidate is stale or does not fit, the reply is held before dispatch; cancel that ready account, adjust supported resources or choose a suitable connection, and prepare again. A single source exchange larger than the recorder's window may require selected passages. Nothing silently deletes the newer tail to fit.

## Resources and text

Settings → Resources applies to a desk's later replies. Reset restores the displayed defaults. Maximum reply tokens is optional: blank uses the connection default; the included Qwen defaults to 2,048 output tokens in an 8,192-token window. Codex's bridge does not expose a hard reply-token cap. A larger output allowance reserves more of a known local window and may leave too little input space. Unsupported values are rejected before dispatch. Short/Medium/Long presets no longer control output.

The plus opens text and image attachments. Text intake defaults to 128 KiB of UTF-8 and can be raised to 1 MiB. The exact attached copy remains saved. Large text initially supplies a bounded opening passage. With a suitable connection, Tools on and the selected-document pocket enabled, a model can read sequential pages (up to 4,000 UTF-16 positions, without splitting surrogate pairs). The default allowance is 32 tool calls and 160,000 bytes of returned evidence per reply. Each page costs a tool call and may require another model response.

For reading a whole document, ask for sequential reading, compact cross-page notes, source ranges and an honest account of unread material. The HTTP loop retains the latest full page, earlier source handles and the model's intervening notes; exact earlier pages remain in receipts and can be reopened. It does not infer that delivery proves understanding. A window, timeout or call limit can still interrupt reading. Attached-copy details show delivered range coverage; Reply details shows the exact page results and nextOffset. Resume explicitly from that offset in another reply. When tools are unavailable, select a passage or use a suitable connection; an opening excerpt is not a whole-document review.

Stop this episode cancels work for the selected branch or named hearth peer. Stop this hearth cancels both of its peers. Activity → Stop all running episodes (Ctrl+Alt+. while the app is focused) requests cancellation of all app-owned active and queued work, including preparation, and discards pending activation. Automatic settings remain saved; a deliberate new reply or saving Conversation settings resumes ordinary automatic preparation. Hearth resumption remains explicit. Completed history, saved accounts and partial replies remain. Closing an external connection does not prove that a remote provider stopped computing. An unconfirmed save remains visibly labelled; do not assume streamed text was saved until the app confirms it.

## A first visit

The short welcome tour follows Welcome, Personal chair, Dreams, Desks, Toy Shelf, Branches, Coats and Settings. Every step offers Skip tutorial; checking it changes Next to Finish. Escape closes it and Settings → Conversation replays it. Missing controls use previews without creating real conversations or making model calls.

Dreams has a copyable preparation prompt. It prepares a proposed training workflow elsewhere; copying it launches no training and exports no conversation. Image generation, code execution and in-app training remain upcoming where labelled. Independent mobile is a later milestone.

## Safe comparison and rollback

This is a separate unsigned candidate. Check the supplied SHA-256 list and keep earlier packages. Launch with an explicit fresh --data-dir path for a comparison, or make a verified backup first. Do not point an older release at a workspace written by this candidate: new receipt profiles may not be understood. Roll back by opening the older app with its own preserved workspace or a pre-upgrade backup. Uninstalling an app is not permission to delete conversation data, models stored elsewhere or shared prerequisites.

## Coats, archives and continuing hearths

Home → My Coats and Settings → Coats & pockets open the same library and editor. Each saved Personal identity or visiting connection can have a usual exact Coat version. A branch can follow that usual choice or keep its own override; returning to a model restores its override. Existing branch choices are preserved. New branches follow a usual Coat, then a copied desk default, then Conversation; existing retired desks retain their saved starting choices. Saving a newer Coat version alone does not dress any model in it. A running reply keeps the version it started with.

The chat Coat button opens its own branch-specific popout. Settings always opens the general wardrobe, including after switching tabs; unfinished Coat drafts keep their original target. Resize Settings from its lower-right corner or focus that handle and use the arrow keys (Shift makes smaller steps). The chosen width and height survive reopening, while a smaller app window temporarily fits the panel to the available space. Reset size restores the default. Other popouts keep their own size.

Settings → Models calls the account panel “ChatGPT connection & models.” A saved sign-in is reused for a visiting reply. A temporary model-list failure stays distinct from a signed-out account. Local-only startup does not start sign-in. Real subscription reconnection after this upgrade still needs user observation.

Click a sidebar desk heading to fold its branches without navigating away. My Branches offers Add to archive, and Home offers View archives. Restore the parent desk before an archived child. History, drafts and hearths stay saved; active or queued work must stop before archiving. Restoration never starts a model.

Work together at the hearth selects two saved compatible models, including unseated visitors. Each has its own stable episode, Coat and attribution. Calls run sequentially. Peers can help each other, disagree, ask for clarification, return partial work or rest. Send a message to both (default) or one peer; mark shared task changes so both receive them. Quiet waiting makes no model calls. A reply ending or a proposed completion does not end the shared task.

The default allowance is ten minutes, ten total model requests, 40,000 output characters, eight tool calls and 48,000 returned bytes. The explicitly chosen workday preset allows up to eight hours, 80 requests, 320,000 characters, 64 tool calls and 384,000 bytes; each limit can be lowered independently. Both peers, context helpers and unsuccessful attempts share the allowance. Waiting consumes wall-clock time. An explicit extension adds to cumulative usage rather than resetting it. Restart preserves work without waking it automatically.

Longer peer history can use a source-linked helper account; the original context and newer messages remain saved. The helper has no action tools. Reopen H sources in the hearth details or through an enabled source pocket. A failed account or smaller context window holds work without clipping. A saved connection whose destination changes needs a new reviewed task; the previous task remains readable. Two profiles of one local model are two perspectives using the same weights, not different models or concurrent inference.

A saved outside reviewer keeps its writing role if that connection is later moved into a chair. The recorder menu labels that retained choice separately; choosing the chair option switches future preparation to a participant account. Historical accounts keep their original role.

## Closing and unfinished edits

Closing the desktop window waits for the composer, Details and shared-heart
saves. An unfinished custom Coat is kept as a draft in My Coats, without creating
or applying a Coat version. A failed save keeps the window open. Unsent text-file
selections and unfinished agent-profile imports need an explicit discard choice;
they are not silently converted into saved sources. Running episodes are stopped
on close and do not automatically resume on restart.

Background refreshes preserve newer composer text. New handoff preparations bind
the relevant branch journal and MIND account plus shared desk guidance. A sibling
branch's unrelated reflection does not invalidate them. Previously prepared jobs
retain their original binding rules and history.
